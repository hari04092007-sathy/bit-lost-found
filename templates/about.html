About

BIT Lost & Found is a student-built portal for reporting and recovering lost belongings on the Bannari Amman Institute of Technology campus in Sathyamangalam. It is an independent project and is not officially approved or endorsed by the institution.

Only students with a verified @bitsathy.ac.in email can post or claim. Contact details and identifying notes stay private; they are shown only to the reporter, an admin, or a claimant whose claim was approved.

Suggested matches use a simple scoring rule over category, name, description keywords, location and dates. It is not machine learning, so always check the item yourself.
